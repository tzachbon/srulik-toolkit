# Code of Conduct

Srulik Toolkit should be a useful and respectful place to contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Give specific feedback that another contributor can act on.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Keep private information and security reports out of public threads.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not
accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or
other contributions that violate this code. They may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns to
[the repository owner](https://github.com/tzachbon). The maintainer will handle
reports as privately as the circumstances allow.
