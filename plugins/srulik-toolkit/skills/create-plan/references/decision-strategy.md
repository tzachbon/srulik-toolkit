# Decision and strategy planning

Apply these checks to strategic choices, product direction, policy, prioritization, investment decisions, build-vs-buy, organizational choices, and other plans where judgment and tradeoffs dominate execution detail.

## Frame the decision

Establish:

- decision to be made
- objective or value to optimize
- decision owner and stakeholders
- time horizon
- hard constraints and non-negotiables
- reversibility and cost of delay
- evidence threshold needed before commitment

Separate the decision itself from the implementation plan that follows it.

## Research

Inspect:

- current state and baseline performance
- prior decisions and why they were made
- available options, including maintaining the status quo
- customer, user, market, operational, financial, policy, or technical evidence relevant to the choice
- resource and opportunity-cost constraints
- dependencies and second-order effects

Use current evidence when the environment can change materially.

## Grill priorities

Challenge:

- the actual objective rather than the proposed solution
- hidden optimization criteria
- assumptions about users, demand, adoption, cost, timing, or capability
- sunk-cost reasoning
- false binary choices
- unpriced operational burden
- irreversible commitments that could be staged
- stakeholder misalignment
- what evidence would change the preferred option

Use inversion when useful: ask what would make the chosen strategy fail even if execution is competent.

## Compare options

When alternatives are real, compare against explicit drivers. A compact decision matrix may include:

```text
Option | Outcome fit | Evidence | Cost | Time | Risk | Reversibility | Dependencies | Long-term burden
```

Do not convert subjective scores into false precision. Explain decisive tradeoffs in prose.

## Validation

Distinguish:

- decision quality: whether the choice was well-founded given available evidence
- execution quality: whether the plan was carried out correctly
- outcome quality: whether the desired result occurred

Define revisit triggers when the strategy depends on uncertain external conditions.
