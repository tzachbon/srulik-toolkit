# Operations and program planning

Apply these checks to launches, cross-functional programs, recurring operating processes, incident-prevention initiatives, compliance programs, enablement, campaigns, and work coordinated across people or teams.

## Research

Establish:

- desired operating outcome
- current process and baseline
- stakeholders, decision rights, and owners
- dependencies and critical external inputs
- resource/capacity constraints
- calendar constraints and immovable dates
- systems of record and communication channels
- existing metrics, SLAs, policies, or operating cadences
- known failure points and escalation paths

## Grill priorities

Resolve:

- single accountable owner for each outcome
- approval and decision boundaries
- what is actually critical-path versus merely preferred
- capacity assumptions
- service or quality thresholds
- escalation triggers
- launch/go-live criteria
- exception handling
- communication responsibilities
- what becomes recurring after the initial program ends

## Workstream design

Organize around outcomes, not departments. For each workstream define:

- accountable owner or owner type when known
- inputs and dependency date/condition
- output or decision produced
- acceptance evidence
- handoff recipient
- escalation condition

Use milestones only when they represent meaningful decision, integration, launch, or acceptance points.

## Critical path

Identify the dependency chain that determines completion. Distinguish:

- hard dependencies
- soft coordination dependencies
- optional enhancements
- external blockers

Do not assign exact dates unless they are provided or can be verified. If dates are unknown, plan by dependency order and relative milestones.

## Validation

Use operational evidence such as:

- milestone exit criteria
- SLA or quality metrics
- dry runs or rehearsals
- launch readiness checks
- stakeholder acceptance
- process adherence samples
- post-launch health indicators
- ownership transfer confirmation
